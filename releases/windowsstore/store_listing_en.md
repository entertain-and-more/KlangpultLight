## Description
Klangpult light is a streamlined, local-first all-in-one workstation for professional podcast production, voice recording, and media planning on Windows.

Designed as an efficient edition of the modular Klangpult audio system, Klangpult light combines multitrack recording capabilities, an instant soundboard for jingles and sound effects, and a local web planner for structuring episodes and reading scripts.

Highlights:
- Multitrack recording for USB microphones and audio sources
- Integrated low-latency soundboard with automatic audio ducking
- Local web planner companion for episode scripts and storylines
- 100% offline and privacy-first: complete local data control
